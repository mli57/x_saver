# Privacy Policy for X Saver

**Latest Update:** July 8, 2026

## Overview
X Saver is a Chrome extension that allows you to save and organize posts from X. To respect your privacy, this extension does not collect any data. All your data is stored locally on your device only, and is completely separate from X's native bookmarks. X has no visibility into what you're saving with this extension. You can delete your saved posts at any time, and you can export them as a CSV file.

## Data Collection and Storage
- **Saved Posts:** When you click the "+ Save" button underneath each post, X Saver stores the following information locally on your device:
  - Post author name
  - Post text content
  - Post timestamp
  - Post URL
  - Timestamp of when you saved the post

- **Storage Location:** All data is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). No data is sent to external servers or cloud services.

- **Badge Count:** Your browser stores the count of saved posts to display on the extension icon. This is also stored locally only.

## Data Sharing & X Account Integration
X Saver **does not:**
- Share your saved posts with anyone
- Send data to external servers
- Collect analytics or usage statistics
- Track your browsing behavior
- Store data in the cloud
- Sell or monetize your data
- Sync your saved posts to your X account or bookmarks
- Allow X to see what posts you're saving with this extension

Your X Saver collection is completely separate from X's native bookmarks and remains private to your device.

## Export Feature
When you use the "export posts" feature, X Saver generates a CSV file that is downloaded directly to your computer. This export is not sent anywhere and remains entirely under your control.

## Permissions
X Saver requests the following permissions:

- **storage**: To save and retrieve your posts from local browser storage
- **tabs**: To open saved posts in new tabs when you click on them

None of these permissions allow X Saver to access your browsing history, other websites, or your X account.

## Changes to This Policy
If any changes are made to this privacy policy, I will update this document will be accordingly.

## Questions or Concerns
If you have questions about this privacy policy or X Saver's handling of your data or want to report a bug, please open an issue on my GitHub repository.

## Data Deletion
You can delete all saved posts at any time using the "Clear all" button in the extension popup. This permanently removes all data from your device, so make sure to export any data you need beforehand.
